# Rakhi Embrace

Please overhaul the "Virtual Raksha Bandhan Ceremony" website to make it deeply emotional, cinematic, and interactive with smooth animations, authentic traditions, and natural heartfelt English captions.

### 🌟 1. Flow & Interactive Steps

1. **Welcome Screen:**

   - URL-based personalization (`?name=BrotherName` or an input box where I enter his name).

   - Display a cute animated sister avatar who waves and says: *"Hello my dearest Brother! Even though miles separate us today, our bond is unbreakable. Shall we celebrate our Rakhi ceremony together?"*

   - Interactive choice buttons:

     - `[Yes, let's celebrate! 💖]` -> Proceeds to the ceremony.

     - `[No / Maybe later 🥺]` -> Playful sister reaction: *"No isn't an option when it comes to your sister! Come on, let's tie your Rakhi!"* and redirects to Yes.

2. **Step 1: Tilak / Bottu Ceremony (Interactive Design Choice):**

   - Display the brother's photo/frame in the center.

   - Show a palette of Tilak/Bottu styles for him to choose:

     1. Traditional Round Red Kumkum with Akshat (Rice grains)

     2. Elegant Chandan (Sandalwood) & Red Tilak

     3. Royal Trishul / Festive Tilak

   - *Interaction:* When selected, the sister's hand/avatar animates forward to place the chosen Tilak gently on the forehead of the photo, accompanied by a glowing sparkle effect and a warm caption: *"Applying the sacred tilak for your long life, prosperity, and endless happiness."*

3. **Step 2: Aarti / Harathi Blessing:**

   - Display an ornate golden Aarti Thali with a glowing diya, flowers, and incense smoke particles.

   - *Interaction:* The user drags the plate in a circular clockwise motion (or clicks `[Perform Aarti]`), triggering a 360-degree rotational animation around his photo with soft temple bell sound effects/visual glow.

   - *Caption:* *"Circling the divine light to protect you from all obstacles and illuminate your path."*

4. **Step 3: Select & Tie the Rakhi:**

   - Show a showcase of 4–5 beautifully designed Rakhis (e.g., Traditional Zari, Rudraksha, Modern Minimalist Gold, Colorful Floral).

   - Once chosen, animate a close-up of a wrist where the selected Rakhi wraps around and ties its golden threads with a subtle pulse/sparkle.

   - *Caption:* *"Tying this sacred thread of eternal love, trust, and sisterly protection around your wrist."*

5. **Step 4: Feeding the Sweet:**

   - Present a sweet box selection (Kaju Katli, Gulab Jamun, Motichoor Laddu, Rasgulla).

   - When selected, the sweet lifts up and animates smoothly toward the brother's mouth with a "Munch/Delicious" sparkle effect and confetti.

   - *Caption:* *"A bite of sweetness to celebrate our sweetest memories and endless laughter!"*

6. **Step 5: The Virtual Warm Hug & Blessings:**

   - Transition to a heartwarming screen where the animated sister avatar moves in for a warm, screen-filling hug animation (arms wrapping, pulsing glowing heart, floating festive particles) that truly feels like an embrace.

   - *Caption:* *"Sending you the tightest, warmest hug across all the miles. Always stay safe and blessed, my dearest Annayya/Brother."*

   - Add an interactive button: `[Touch Sister's Head for Blessings 🙏]` which reveals a heartfelt message: *"Thank you for always being my pillar of strength, my secret-keeper, and my forever protector."*

7. **Grand Finale & Gift / Letter Note:**

   - Festive fireworks/confetti burst across the screen.

   - Display a personalized parchment letter with the message:

     *"No distance can ever weaken our bond. Happy Raksha Bandhan! 💖"*

   - Buttons: `[Download Celebration Memory Card (PNG/PDF)]` and `[Share on WhatsApp / Replay]`.

---

### 🎨 Visual & Audio Styling

- **Color Palette:** Festive Indian Royal theme (Deep Maroon/Crimson, Warm Gold, Ivory, soft festive orange glows).

- **Audio/Feedback:** Soft optional festive instrumental shehnai/sitar background music with a mute/unmute toggle.

- **Tone & Copy:** Warm, poetic, and loving conversational English (avoid rigid literal labels like "Show Harathi").
